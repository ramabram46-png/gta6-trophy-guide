Road to the platinum

Three stages, from PowerPyx. The checklist in stage 2 is closer to 60% of true completion — most thresholds are partial, and only a few lines need everything.

    Cheats cost you the platinum. Using any of the 61 cheats disables trophies and saving for the session, and none of them unlock before the credits.

    Keep chapter saves. There is no mission replay: 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start and one at the end of each chapter, then rotate the rest for cleanup.
